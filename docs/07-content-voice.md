# 07 — Voice and Content

## 1. The sentence shape

Apple: fragment, period, fragment.

Roots and Mills:

> Haldi. Ground for colour. Kept for calm.

Not:

> Discover our premium authentic turmeric powder that will take your dishes to the next level!

## 2. Words we use

Mastery, mill, lot, garden, press, grind, leaf, origin, kitchen, tin, bag, bottle, quiet, considered, dummy mill lot (until live).

Sanskrit triad: always those spellings — **Dakshya, Prateet, Soma**.

## 3. Words we avoid

Unlock, journey, passion, synergy, farm-to-fork (cliché unless proven), 100% authentic (empty), limited time (unless true), slay, addictive.

## 4. Microcopy

| Place | Copy |
|---|---|
| Add to bag | Add to bag |

| Empty bag | The mill is quiet. Add a tin. |
| Empty search | Nothing in the mill for that. |

## 5. Review prompts (later)

Ask for: aroma, colour, how they cooked, would they restock.  
Do not ask for “rate your experience 1-10” only.

## 6. Legal tone

PDM Enterprises is the seller. Roots and Mills is the brand. Invoices: PDM Enterprises.
